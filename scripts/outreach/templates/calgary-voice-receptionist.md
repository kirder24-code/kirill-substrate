## HOW TO FIND TARGETS (20 min manual research):
1. Google Maps: "dental clinic Calgary", "physiotherapy Calgary", "optometry Calgary" - filter 3.5-4.5 stars
2. Visit website - look for owner/clinic director name on "About" or "Team" page
3. Find direct email: firstname@clinic.ca or info@clinic.ca (personalize with owner name in greeting)
4. SKIP if: national chain (Dentalcorp, Shoppers Drug Mart clinic), no owner name findable
5. Priority signals: "new patients welcome", "book online" not available, Google reviews mentioning "hard to reach" or "phone tag"

## BEST CURRENT TARGETS:
- Independent dental clinics (1-3 dentists) - highest missed-call rate, appointment-critical
- Physiotherapy / chiropractic - high call volume, insurance coordination calls stack up
- Optometry - seasonal booking surges (back-to-school, year-end benefits)
- Veterinary clinics - clients call when panicked, missed = client lost permanently
