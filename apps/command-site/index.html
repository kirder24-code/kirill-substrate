THE PROBLEM

Your business generates more data than you can act on.

01

Data without intelligence

You have the numbers. You don't have the signal. What does a 3% revenue dip actually mean, and what should you do about it by noon today?

02

Decisions made too late

By the time information reaches you - through meetings, reports, updates - the window has already passed. Markets, leads, opportunities don't wait.

03

Everything is disconnected

Finance in one tool, operations in another, sales in a third, HR in a fourth. No single view. No single brain making sense of it all.

THE PRODUCT

COMMAND - Your AI Operating System

One dashboard. Your entire business.

COMMAND gives you a real-time view of every vertical - revenue, operations, marketing, HR, technology - in a single screen designed specifically for how you think about your business.

  • Custom-branded to your company - not a generic BI tool
  • Live metrics from your actual data sources (CRM, ERP, spreadsheets)
  • AI-generated morning briefing - decisions due, signals, risks
  • Mobile PWA - your command centre in your pocket
  • Role-based access - different views for CEO, COO, CFO

// AI MORNING BRIEF · SYNTHESISED

Good morning. Revenue is up 2.4% WoW. Three contracts due for renewal by Friday. Your top sales lead has gone cold - reach out today. Supply chain flag: lead times extended by 12 days.

Specialised intelligence. One module per function.

Each AI module owns a function of your business - monitoring, analysing, flagging, acting - so you focus on decisions, not information gathering.

  • Finance module: P&L monitoring, cash flow signals, deal tracking
  • Sales module: pipeline health, follow-up reminders, win/loss patterns
  • Operations module: supply chain, vendor performance, delivery tracking
  • Marketing module: content pipeline, campaign performance, social metrics
  • Modules named and themed to your brand - not generic chatbots
