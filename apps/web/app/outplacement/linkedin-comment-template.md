# LinkedIn Comment Templates - Outplacement Firms

Use when a firm leader posts about Q1 layoff surge volume, consultant capacity strain, or AI disruption anxiety.
Diagnose one specific operational pain point. End with a soft question. No pitch.

---

## Template 1 - Q1 Layoff Surge / Volume Spike

> The surge volume piece is real. What usually breaks first is the intake side - when new candidates arrive faster than consultants can manually onboard them, the first 2-3 weeks of each engagement get eaten by data entry and missing-field chasing instead of actual coaching. The number of active candidates per consultant becomes almost irrelevant because the intake queue itself becomes the ceiling. Are you seeing the bottleneck show up earlier in the process, or more at the job-sourcing and tracking stage?

---

## Template 2 - Capacity Strain / Consultant Bandwidth

> The bandwidth problem compounds in an interesting way - it is not just the headcount that limits scale, it is the daily admin that grows linearly with every new candidate added. Job-board sourcing alone at 50+ active candidates per consultant can run 3-4 hours a day before any coaching work starts. The consultants are still doing the same manual search-and-screenshot process they used at 20 candidates. At what point does the ops side of the role start to crowd out the work clients actually hired you for?

---

## Template 3 - AI Disruption Anxiety / Positioning

> The anxiety makes sense when most of what gets called "AI for outplacement" is chatbot-layer stuff that does not touch the actual bottleneck. The real capacity drain is not candidate communication - it is the back-office: intake processing, job-match sourcing, and queue tracking that runs manually across spreadsheets for every consultant. Those workflows are automatable today without touching the coaching relationship at all. Curious what specific part of your operation you are most focused on - is it the intake side, the job-sourcing, or the reporting layer?

---

## Usage Notes

- Post must be from a decision-maker at an outplacement firm (Director, VP, CEO, Head of Operations)
- Post must be published within 7 days - check post date before commenting
- Do not send a DM immediately after commenting - wait for a reply first
- If they reply to the comment, follow up with one clarifying question before offering a call
